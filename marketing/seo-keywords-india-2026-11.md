# India keyword research — November 2026

**Data:** SE Ranking, India database, pulled 2026-11-08. About 175 keywords checked.

**How to read it:** volume is SE Ranking's estimate of monthly searches in India, and its figures are rounded and sometimes inflated. Difficulty runs from 0 to 100 and is SE Ranking's own score. With 3 linking domains, PDF Section can realistically aim at difficulty below about 50 for now. That cut-off is a rule of thumb, not something the data proves.

Hinglish searches, such as "pdf ka size kaise kam kare" ("how to reduce PDF size"), mostly returned no data. That doesn't prove nobody searches them; SE Ranking may just not track them well.

## Product opportunity

The biggest India demand is for exact sizes: "compress pdf to 200kb" (246,000), "compress pdf to 100kb" (135,000) and "jpg to pdf 100kb" (5,400). Neither Compress PDF nor JPG to PDF lets you pick a target size; they only offer presets.

A "make it under ___ KB" option would fit what these searchers want much better than any page copy could. It would also make your existing "compress to 100KB/200KB" pages honest about what they deliver. This is a product change, not marketing, so it's the founder's call.
